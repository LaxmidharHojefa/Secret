This folder is for your video memories.

Expected filenames:
  our-memory-01.mp4   — A video memory or moment

Guidelines:
- MP4 format recommended for maximum browser compatibility.
- If a video is missing, the player simply won't load (no broken UI).
- Keep videos under 50MB. For longer videos, consider using a streaming service and embedding externally.
- You can add more videos by editing src/components/MemoryGallery.tsx and adding entries to the VIDEOS array.
